# Code of Conduct

LechYTDLP welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, including when someone officially represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Welcome questions, different viewpoints, and contributions from newcomers.
- Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For abuse on GitHub, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). To reach the project maintainer, ask [@lechixy](https://github.com/lechixy) for a private contact method. Keep names, evidence, and details out of public posts.

Maintainers may remove or edit content, limit participation, or block accounts based on the severity and frequency of the behavior. They will handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to address the incident.
